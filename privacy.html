Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

vibecoding ("the App") is a personal command-line tool maintained by Micah Alpern. It is used by its operator to access their own Google account data through the Google APIs. This policy explains how the App handles that data.

What the App accesses

When you authorize the App, it requests OAuth access to your own Google Workspace data — which may include Gmail messages and metadata, Calendar, Drive files, Contacts, Tasks, and related services — solely to perform the actions you invoke from the command line on your own device.

How data is used

  • All processing happens locally on the operator's own machine(s). The App reads and acts on data only to fulfill commands the operator runs.
  • OAuth credentials (refresh/access tokens) are stored locally on the operator's device, protected by filesystem permissions.
  • The App does not operate any backend server, and does not transmit your Google data to the developer or to any third party.

Data sharing & sale

Your Google user data is never sold and never shared with third parties. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the operator's own direct command-line use.

Limited Use

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Data retention & deletion

The App retains no data beyond what exists transiently in memory during a command and the locally stored OAuth tokens. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and local tokens can be removed by deleting the App's configuration directory.
